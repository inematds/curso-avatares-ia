How to study

Read in short sessions and do the practice in the suggested time. Progress stays in this browser. Make copies using the journey’s export option.

Lesson images are illustrations generated in Codex, not video results. To complete production, you need access to the chosen voice and avatar tools with the relevant features. Check credits and features before starting.

The Oficina Clara case

Bia, a producer, and Otávio, an instructor, plan presentations for a workshop. There are two paths: represent the teacher himself or create Nina, a virtual presenter. Apply the exercises to your own identity or an invented character.

Deliverables include a HeyGen test, a line in Kling, and a motion test. The four-episode series requires extra production. Check access before starting and leave uncompleted steps pending.

Prerequisite: know how to save files and make a simple video. If needed, start with Your First AI Video.

Who it is for

It is for you if you

  • You have made a simple video and want more control over tests.
  • You have your own references and are willing to review results and costs.
  • Prefer to learn by doing.

It is not for you if you

  • Already know the subject well and want technical material.
  • Want a traditional video lesson.
